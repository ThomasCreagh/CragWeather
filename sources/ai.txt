├── package.json
└── server.js                # Express entry point

How it works:

    server.js — Starts Express, serves public/, mounts API routes.

    routes/ — Defines endpoints (GET /api/users, etc.).

    controllers/ — Handles endpoint logic.

    public/ — Frontend HTML, CSS and Vue.

    services/api.js — Frontend fetch() calls to Express.

    components/ — Vue components using plain JavaScript.

Without Vite: Load Vue 3 via CDN in index.html, using ES modules for your own JavaScript files.

For fewer than 10 API endpoints, you could simplify further by merging routes/api.js and controllers/apiController.js into one file.

give me potential health risks associated with those levels being above their good limits

Potential health risks when air pollutants exceed their recommended limits:

Pollutant
	

Potential health risks

CO (Carbon monoxide)
	

Headaches, dizziness, nausea, fatigue, reduced oxygen delivery to organs. Severe exposure can cause unconsciousness or death.

NO₂ (Nitrogen dioxide)
	

Airway inflammation, coughing, wheezing, shortness of breath, worsened asthma, increased respiratory infections.

O₃ (Ozone)
	

Chest pain, throat irritation, coughing, reduced lung function, worsened asthma and other respiratory diseases.

SO₂ (Sulfur dioxide)
	

Breathing difficulties, throat irritation, bronchospasm, asthma attacks, especially in sensitive individuals.

PM2.5 (Fine particles)
	

Lung inflammation, worsened asthma, increased risk of heart attacks, strokes, cardiovascular disease and premature death with prolonged exposure.

PM10 (Inhalable particles)
	

Eye and throat irritation, coughing, aggravated asthma and bronchitis, reduced lung function and increased respiratory problems.

Important: Exceeding a guideline does not mean these symptoms will immediately occur. Risk depends on concentration, exposure duration and individual health.

Children, older adults, pregnant people, and those with heart or lung conditions are generally more vulnerable.
